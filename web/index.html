Knock Knock Imposter

The sun kills anyone outside after dawn. Tonight, someone is knocking.

  • Look through the peephole and talk to them. Interrupt whenever you like: they stop when you speak.
  • Some visitors are human. Some only sound human. Push them, and the imitation slips.
  • But push a real person too hard, and they start to sound wrong too.

Use headphones. Allow the microphone.
